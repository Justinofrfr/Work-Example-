# Pet Game Picks — fact-checked (2026-09-30)

How this was made: 148 pet ideas (128 new + 20 from the earlier pool) → 60 fact-checked by agents (≤3 Roblox searches each) → top ideas re-checked by hand → 10 picks. Roblox APIs are blocked in this environment, so competitor numbers come from web-search snippets (Rolimons/Roblox pages, code sites, press). Search Roblox yourself the day you start a build.

## Why pets, and why not "merge pets"
- Pets are hot: Steal An Egg ~3.1B visits in 2 months; Catch And Tame 430M; Adopt Me, Build a Zoo, Pets Universe, Ride a Pet all shipped Sept 2026 updates.
- It is also the most crowded lane on Roblox. Taken loops: hatch → equip → multiplier (Pet Sim 99 + hundreds), Steal a X, adopt/RP, lasso → breed → mutate (Catch And Tame), merge pets (Merge Pets!, Pet Merge Simulator!, Merge Your Pets, +1 Pet Merge, Merge The Kittens 11M, Merge Vs Mobs 14M), zoo builders, pet daycare (Animal Daycare 16K peak CCU), dog walking (Dog Walk 403M), dog racing (Dog Race 28M), cat hide-and-seek (Play Cats 19M), blind-guide obby (Tell Me! 73M).
- What is still open: what the pet DOES, how you GET it, and what happens when you are OFFLINE. Every pick below changes one of those.

## Pick cards

### 1. Keep It Alive — BUILD
*Merges PET-001 + PMX-031 Raise a Bloodline.*
- **30s promise:** an egg hatches, it's hungry, and the timer says it can actually die.
- **Loop:** Minute: feed, clean, play, teach tricks (each fills a meter). Session: unlock rooms, breed a mate. Day: hunger/sleep cycles run offline (safe for a sleep cycle; danger starts after ~20h absence). Week: Saturday species drops, "Oldest Living" leaderboard reset season.
- **Twist:** neglect it long enough and it dies → gravestone garden on your plot; its children carry its traits and a family tree. The showoff is age (a 30-day-old pet is rarer than any drop) and lineage depth.
- **Painkillers:** Medicine (revive within 24h of death, 49 R$ — fires at the death screen) · Auto-Feeder 3 days (39 R$ — fires on logout) · Lifespan Elixir (+3 days max age, 29 R$) · Egg Luck (odds shown) · 2× meters pass.
- **Clip moment:** the "day 30" birthday party vs the gravestone reveal.
- **Returns:** D1 hunger timer · D7 first breeding · D28 lineage/age leaderboard.
- **Safety framing:** death is soft (pet "goes to the Rainbow Garden", can be revived, kids never see suffering). Show the meters clearly so it never feels unfair.
- **MVP:** one plot, 12 species, 3 rooms, breeding with 2 visible traits. Cut trading.
- **Kill if:** testers feel cheated by a death (adjust timers) or nobody cares about age.
- **Risk:** a dead pet churns casual kids. Mitigation: first pet cannot die for 7 days; medicine is cheap.

### 2. Raise the Server Baby — BUILD
*MSH-086.*
- **30s promise:** a giant baby creature sits in the middle of the map and everyone is feeding it.
- **Loop:** Minute: gather food/toys, feed and play with the server baby, earn coins. Session: its meters cross thresholds → it grows a stage (cutscene). Day: it evolves every ~6h based on what the server fed it (meat → beast, candy → sugar blob, veggies → tree spirit); a new baby every day. Week: a Saturday legendary egg.
- **Twist:** ONE pet per server, 100 players, and its form is a vote made with food. Top feeders get the "Godparent" chest at each evolution.
- **Painkillers:** Golden Bottle (your feed counts ×5 for 10 min, 39 R$) · Godparent Chest ×2 (49 R$, at the evolution countdown) · Summon Rare Egg (server, 149 R$ — flex) · Personal mini-pet passes.
- **Clip moment:** the evolution reveal — the whole server crowds round as it turns into something nobody expected.
- **Returns:** D1 next evolution timer · D7 evolution book (collect every form) · D28 season eggs.
- **MVP:** one map, one baby with 4 stages × 3 branches (12 forms), food scaled to CCU, bots feed in empty servers.
- **Kill if:** solo players leave before the first evolution.
- **Risk:** freeloaders; solve with contribution-scaled rewards.

### 3. Regrow Your Axolotl — BUILD
*PET-017.*
- **30s promise:** your axolotl loses a leg, and it grows back… glowing.
- **Loop:** Minute: swim your axolotl through tank hazards (crabs, currents, other players' axolotls nip); losing a limb starts a regrow timer; the regrown part rolls a mutation (albino, gold, galaxy, crystal). Session: tank upgrades, water temperature (cold = slower but rarer regrows). Day: regrow timers (10–60 min, offline), tank restock. Week: Saturday mutation lines.
- **Twist:** damage IS the gacha. You *want* to get bitten. Tank PvP (nipping) becomes a favour between friends.
- **Painkillers:** Regrow Serum (skip timer, 19–79 R$ — fires at the timer) · Mutation Water (luck, odds shown, 49 R$) · Extra Tank Slot (149 R$) · Friend Nip Boost.
- **Clip moment:** a full-body regrow into a galaxy axolotl.
- **Returns:** D1 regrow timers · D7 mutation index · D28 body-set completion (all 6 parts one theme).
- **MVP:** one tank, 8 hazards, 5 body parts × 12 mutations. Cut trading.
- **Kill if:** kids find the limb loss upsetting (test the art: cartoon pop-off, no blood).
- **Risk:** Roblox moderation on "dismemberment" — keep it cute and bloodless.

### 4. Befriend the Thing Under the Bed — BUILD (ship before Oct 23)
*SSN-047.*
- **30s promise:** put a night-light by your bed and something small and fuzzy peeks out.
- **Loop:** Minute: decorate your room (lamps, snacks, socks, books); each item changes which creature spawns at "night" (every 4 min). Sit still, then befriend it with the right offering. Session: collect creatures, they become room pets with passive coins. Day: real night cycle + offline visits ("something rearranged your shelf"). Week: Saturday new creature families.
- **Twist:** decor is the lure; collection is the reward; cozy-spooky, not horror.
- **Painkillers:** Rare Lure candle (luck, odds shown, 39 R$) · Night Skip (19 R$) · Extra Room (149 R$) · Offline visits ×2.
- **Clip moment:** a giant fluffy creature squeezing out from under a tiny bed.
- **Returns:** D1 night cycle · D7 creature book · D28 Halloween→Christmas creature seasons.
- **MVP:** one room, 25 creatures, 40 decor items. Cut trading.
- **Kill if:** testers can't figure out the lure rules (add hint cards).

### 5. Win the Ugly Dog Contest — BUILD
*PET-060.*
- **30s promise:** breed two dogs and the puppy is hilariously wrong.
- **Loop:** Minute: breed, feed, groom (badly on purpose). Session: enter the hourly Ugly Contest; server votes; winner's genes become a public stud. Day: contest every hour, daily theme (ugliest tongue, weirdest ears). Week: Saturday World Championship.
- **Twist:** reverse beauty — the same 2-trait genetics as cute games, but voting decides value.
- **Painkillers:** Mutation Kibble (luck, 39 R$) · Instant Litter (skip pregnancy timer, 29 R$) · Contest Re-entry (19 R$) · Stud Pass.
- **Clip moment:** the reveal of a 1-in-10,000 "Perfectly Ugly" puppy.
- **Returns:** D1 hourly contest · D7 breed book · D28 championship ladder.
- **MVP:** 10 body parts × 6 variants, contest room, voting UI, bots vote in small servers.
- **Kill if:** votes are random (add "vote for the ugliest = +coins for accuracy").

### 6. Read Your Pet's Diary — BUILD
*PMX-002.* Your pet's offline hours are simulated against other players' pets in a shared neighbourhood (made friends with X's cat, stole Y's sock, got stuck in a tree). On return you get a 3-panel diary and the items it found. Money: Adventure Pack (better offline events), Alarm Collar (skip bad events), extra pet slots. Risk: needs a good event writer; cheap to script.

### 7. Fetch Across the Server — BUILD (fast)
*PMX-043.* Every throw is a server scramble; dogs have 2 visible traits (speed, jump) and can return other players' throws for a bonus. 2-week build, MID money (dog eggs with luck, throw boosts, ball skins). Good "learn to launch" game.

### 8. Sit a Stranger's Pet — PIVOT
*PET-029.* Keep it positive-only: sitting can only help a pet, never harm it. Money: sitter licences, tip jars, premium food. Strong social hook, MID money.

### 9. Trim a Hoof — BUILD (niche)
*HOB-067.* 60-second single-station ASMR loop; rare show horses as clients. Zero occupants, proven 273M-view video demand. Small ceiling, small build.

### 10. Wash a Muddy Dragon — PIVOT
*MSH-016.* Scrub-to-reveal genetics plus blind muddy trades; lead the thumbnail with the reveal, not the washing.

## Build order for pets
1. **Keep It Alive** — biggest real-world trend (Tamagotchi 100M, 30th anniversary in 2026), open lane, highest money (medicine/auto-feeder are perfect painkillers).
2. **Regrow Your Axolotl** — Gen Alpha's favourite animal, dead lane, cheapest gacha to build.
3. **Raise the Server Baby** — best co-play signal; build it once you have an audience to fill servers.
Ship **Befriend the Thing Under the Bed** instead of #3 if you can launch before Oct 23 (Halloween Spotlight window).
