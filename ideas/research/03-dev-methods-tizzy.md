# How successful devs find ideas — Jul–Sep 2026 (researched 2026-09-27)
Sources: unofficial distillation of 21 Tizzy RBLX videos (github.com/Ticklect/tizzy-rblx-advisor, pub. 2026-09-27), search summaries; YouTube/DevForum/Reddit direct fetch blocked.

## Checklist
1. Research OFF Roblox first — "none of it happens on Roblox" (Tizzy, "I paid a top dev $300…", May 19 2026). YouTube, TikTok, Steam, CrazyGames, mobile, Minecraft/GMod.
2. Scout small rising games, not the front page: CCU vs visits, age, trend, update cadence, complaints — then play 2+ hours.
3. Find proven demand with poor execution (Tizzy: Alien Ranch → his Farmer Die).
4. Combine a proven fantasy with a new loop / move a loop to a new theme / revive an old dead loop.
5. The twist must change a real building block (fantasy, tension, social structure, risk/reward) — not just the theme.
6. Filter by dev time vs demand vs competition.
7. Mashups are proven now: Animal Hospital = Scary Shawarma Kiosk + 99 Nights + Phasmophobia → reported ~1.2M CCU peak.
8. RDC 2026 (Sep 10–12): ranking now age-aware (younger players favor short-form), first-28-days optimisation, "direct growth" signal being tested; Moments video clips expanding.
9. RoTrends reportedly deprecated Aug 31 2026 (unverified) → use RoVitals, RoWatcher, Rolimons, rblxdb.
