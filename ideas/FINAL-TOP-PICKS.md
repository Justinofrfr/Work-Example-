# Final Top Picks — Judge's Ruling (2026-09-27)

## How this list was made
- **Funnel:** 1,070 generated ideas → 1,043 unique after de-duplication → 235 fact-checked (Roblox occupancy, demand, money density, red flags) → 40 finalists → **14 picks** after merging duplicates and applying the five idea gates from the rulebook.
- **Rules applied:** a FAIL on gate 1 (original at scale) or gate 5 (buildable ≤1 month + killable) rules out PURSUE. PURSUE also requires no FAIL on any gate and at most 2 WEAK. Arguments that rested on unsourced numbers were thrown out. Ideas whose twist is **mechanical** (social friction, co-op or PvP) rank above ideas that only change the theme, because theme lanes filled within weeks (geode, keyboard and star went from open in mid-Aug to crowded by late Sep 2026).
- **Money density → net $/CCU-month** (Investor memo, estimates): HIGH ≈ $1.9–4.2 · MID ≈ $1.5–2.3 · LOW/party ≈ $0.6.
- **Data caveats:** Roblox APIs and Rolimons were blocked by the network, so all occupancy figures come from **web-search snippets**. "None found" does not prove a lane is open. Some trend numbers come from secondary trend blogs (marked). **Search Roblox yourself (game search + Rolimons) the day you start building each pick.** If a live competitor with more than 1M visits has appeared, go back to the Judge before continuing.
- **Dev assets:** you already own a peg-board ball-drop system and a promo-code system. Never frame the peg board as "Plinko" or a casino, because the word is censored and it looks like gambling. Present it as a *machine* (sorter, chute, tumbler), feed it soft currency only, and let Robux buy luck/speed, never drops.
- **Speed bet** = the lane can fill in weeks; ship the MVP in 2–3 weeks or drop it.
