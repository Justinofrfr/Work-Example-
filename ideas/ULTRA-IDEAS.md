## Pick cards (what to build, what to cut, what sells)

### 1. Keep It Alive
- **First 30s:** egg hatches, meters appear, the timer says it can actually die.
- **Loop:** feed/clean/play → rooms and toys → breed a mate (2 visible traits). Eggs incubate; open EARLY for a MEGA/shiny chance at the risk of a dud (friends at your incubator add luck). Pets keep living while you're offline in a shared neighbourhood: any passer-by can feed yours (they earn coins + reputation), and your diary shows who saved it. Nobody helps → it goes to the Rainbow Garden (revivable 24h); its kids inherit its traits.
- **Sells:** Medicine (death screen) · Stable Shell (before an early open) · Auto-Feeder 3 days (logout) · Lifespan Elixir · egg luck (odds shown).
- **Returns:** D1 hunger timer + "who fed my pet" · D7 first litter · D28 Oldest Pet + Most Rescues boards.
- **MVP cut:** 12 species, one plot, sitting is server-local (no cross-server writes), no trading. First pet can't die for 7 days.
- **Kill if:** testers feel cheated by a death, or nobody feeds strangers' pets.

### 2. Steal a Charm
- **First 30s:** roll a capsule, a plush charm clips to your backpack, coins tick.
- **Loop:** capsule machines restock every 15 min (limited stock); charms earn Style/sec and add weight; hourly Bag Race (heavy = flashy but slow). Every 10 min ONE Golden Charm drops: grab it → glow + ×10 luck → anyone can tag-steal → hold it 60s at the Vault to bank a permanent Mythic.
- **Sells:** Escort Shield 8s (fires when you grab gold) · Lucky Spray (restock) · Featherweight (race lobby) · +Bag Slots · UGC charm kiosk (you keep 40%).
- **MVP cut:** 30 original charms, one mall, NPC "shoppers" fill the chase in small servers, no trading.
- **Kill if:** nobody chases the gold holder.

### 3. Raise the Baby
- **First 30s:** a giant baby creature in the middle of the map, 40 players feeding it.
- **Loop:** gather food/toys → feed → meters cross thresholds → evolution cutscene every ~6h; food type picks the branch (meat/candy/veg → 3 forms × 4 stages). Neglect → grumpy: it chases anyone standing alone (stay near others). Prolonged neglect → it dies, funeral cutscene, new egg. Top carers get a mini-offspring pet.
- **Sells:** Golden Bottle (×5 feed, 10 min) · Godparent Chest ×2 (evolution countdown) · Server Medicine (revive at the funeral) · Summon Rare Egg (server flex).
- **MVP cut:** one map, 12 forms, food scaled to CCU, bots feed in empty servers, no offspring pets until update 2.
- **Kill if:** solo players leave before the first evolution.

### 4. Take One Candy
- **First 30s:** a porch, a bowl, a sign: TAKE ONE. Take one, or grab the bowl.
- **Loop:** walk a Halloween street; every porch bowl restocks from a shared server pool; take one (safe, small) or steal the bowl (big, but the doorbell cam posts your face on the street's Wall of Shame and homeowners get a bounty on you). Candy has rarity; a live price board changes with how much the server stole vs. left.
- **Sells:** Disguise mask (before a steal) · Cam Jammer · Restock Now (server) · candy luck.
- **MVP cut:** one street of 12 houses, 20 candies, NPC homeowners.
- **Kill if:** everyone steals and nobody cares about shame (raise bounties) — or the reverse.

### 5. Freeze Dry It!
- **First 30s:** candy in, door open, a crunchy puff twice the size.
- **Loop:** shop restocks every 5 min → load trays → timers (offline) → open the door early for a MEGA at collapse risk; friends holding the door add luck. Every hour a ×10 MEGA-luck window spawns at one player's dryer and can be tag-stolen (the Golden Ticket engine, no factory level). Sell at your stand, display rares.
- **Sells:** Stable Door (before an early open) · Instant Dry · Mutation Syrup · Sugar Rush (server) · +Dryer.
- **MVP cut:** one kitchen, 12 candies, 4 mutations, no trading. Halloween candy first, Christmas next.
- **Kill if:** testers stop after their first puff or never open early.

### 6. Lure a Critter
- **Loop:** yard by day (feeders/seed → birds), room by night (lamp/snack/sock → cozy creatures); every item shifts spawn odds; befriended critters become pets earning coins; NPC raccoons raid at night (baffle repair). Offline visitor diary on return.
- **Sells:** Rare Lure candle (odds shown) · Premium Seed · Night Skip · Baffle Repair · 24h Offline Camera.
- **MVP cut:** 25 critters, 40 decor items, NPC raids only.

### 7. Regrow My Axolotl
- **Loop:** swim through tank hazards; losing a limb starts a regrow timer and rolls a mutation; friends' axolotls nip yours as a favour; every 10 min a server Feeding Frenzy (most pellets = guaranteed rare regrow).
- **Sells:** Regrow Serum (timer) · Mutation Water (odds shown) · Extra Tank · Frenzy Boost.
- **MVP cut:** no breeding until month 2. Cartoon pop-off, no blood.

### 8. Wash the Kaiju
- **Loop:** 100 players pressure-wash one giant sleeping monster (zones pay, barnacles roll treasure) → it wakes (cutscene) → 3-minute feed phase → the food mix picks its colour/size variant → Wake Chest to top contributors → next kaiju.
- **Sells:** Turbo Nozzle · Treasure Lens · Wake Chest ×2 · Summon Legendary Kaiju (server).
- **MVP cut:** one kaiju rig with variants (no 3 rigs), no pets, dirt scaled to CCU.

### 9. Sneak Up on It
- **Loop:** wild pets graze; approach downwind, walk slowly, freeze when it looks up; tame = collect. Every hour a Golden spawn: one shared stealth race, and any player who runs scares it off for everyone (server chat: "STOP RUNNING").
- **Sells:** Scent Blocker · Slow-Mo Boots · Golden Radar · net luck.
- **MVP cut:** one meadow, 20 species, 3 behaviours.

### 10. Who Wrecked My House
- **Loop:** build/decorate your house; log off → it stays open; strangers can tidy (coins + rep) or prank (paint, furniture flip) risking the cams; morning report = whodunit with clips; you rate visitors.
- **Sells:** Cam Pack · Cleanup Crew (undo pranks) · Prank Kit · extra rooms.
- **MVP cut:** house state server-local, 10 pranks, 10 chores.

### 11. Bid on Mud Eggs · 12. Raise a Thief Crow · 13. Feed the Bed Monster · 14. Roast a Marshmallow
