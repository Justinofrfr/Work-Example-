- **Drag the Giant Egg** (judge's #1): Pull An Egg (launched Aug 2026) already has you drag giant eggs; snippets show 3.3M+ visits and about 17K playing. That meets the judge's own kill rule ("a pet-tow game with 5K+ players"). Its open slot, whole-server co-op, moves to #2 and #3.

- **Swap Pets Midair / Rob the Egg Train / Tow For Eggs / Cast For Eggs:** crowded X-For-Eggs lane.

## Build order
1. **Ride the Stampede.** It rides the only big pet game still climbing, has proven mass demand from the Rodeo Stampede fantasy, and no Roblox version exists. Timing risk: if Ride A Pet adds a herd event, you're late, so start now.
2. **Build the Nest!** or **Push the Fat Cat** (pick one). Both take the empty "pets + whole-server goal" slot beside Build the Pyramid!. Push the Fat Cat is cheaper. Build the Nest! has the stronger gacha (your haul decides your pet).
3. **Lead the Ducklings.** A new steal mechanic (cut the line) that isn't a Steal An Egg reskin.

**Before starting any of these:** search Roblox + Rolimons for the mechanic and check that the parent game (Ride A Pet, Build the Pyramid!) is still rising. All numbers are web-search snippets and the trackers disagree.

## 1. Is +1 and 99 Nights falling off?

**Yes, both have fallen well below their peaks.** Both are still top-10 by raw player count, so neither is dead. But both are shrinking, and a new game in either format would arrive late.

- **+1 runners are over.**
  - +1 Speed Keyboard Escape leads the format. It has 128K–267K players against a 3.69M–7.26M peak on Jul 18, which is down 93–98%.
  - It is still falling. It had 316K on Sep 5 and 128K–267K in late September. The Sep 19 Admin Abuse event drew about 5.2M RSVPs and did not stop the slide.
  - +1 Speed Monkey Escape has about 49K against a 1.52M all-time high (-97%).
  - Dozens of reskins crowd the category. The Sep 27 market scout puts "+1 Escape clones" on its avoid list.
- **The one exception is +1 as a layer on top of a real second loop.**
  - +1 Loot To Forge has 46K–62K players. It peaked at 80.4K around Sep 28 and is 7 weeks old.
  - +1 Wings For Eggs has 12–14K, and its visits roughly tripled in September.
  - A +1 twist alone no longer brings players. +1 DMG FPS has 1.6K–8.4K despite a 94.9% rating.
- **99 Nights in the Forest has fallen off and now only jumps on update days.**
  - It normally has 104K–260K players against a 14.15M all-time high (Oct 4 2025), which is down more than 98%. It is down 23–69% from about 339K in July 2026.
  - Its 7-day average fell 11% week over week.
  - The Sep 26 update went 282K → 1.55M → 661K within about 3.5 hours, then fell back to 225–260K by Sep 27–30.
  - Every clone (Arctic, Wild West, Forest Roleplay, Zombie Girl) peaked in Aug–Nov 2025 at 29.3K or less. None set a 2026 high.

**Verdict: don't build either one.** The rising formats are below.

## 4. Pick cards

### 1. Drag the Giant Egg
- **First 30 seconds:** A house-sized egg with pets straining on ropes. Press E, it lurches forward, and a shell chunk hatches at your feet.
- **Core loop:** Hatch → train pull → clip on → drag past 3 checkpoints that drop eggs → the Titan hatches a pet for each puller → a bigger Titan spawns.
- **Twist:** One scripted object instead of thousands of blocks, so no lag.
- **Painkillers:** Pull Snack 15R when the egg stalls · Shell Luck 25R as a checkpoint cracks · Catch-Up Rope 10R if you join past 50%.
- **Clip moment:** The Titan cracking open over 40 pets.
- **Returns:** D1 overnight Titan · D7 new route · D28 limited Titan.
- **MVP:** 3 biomes, 12 pets, NPC pullers, pull strength scaled to server size.
- **Kill if:** a pet-tow game has 5K+ players, or Build the Pyramid! adds pets.
- **Risk:** The trend is one game, less than 4 weeks old.

### 2. Climb For Eggs
- **First 30 seconds:** Ride a goat up three glowing ledges. A stranger's gold egg bounces past and you dive to catch it.
- **Core loop:** Climb one shared cliff while your grip drains. Pluck eggs (higher is rarer), bank them at ledges, hatch better climbers, rebirth.
- **Twist:** When you fall, your eggs tumble to whoever is below you.
- **Painkillers:** Chalk Bag 15R when grip turns red · Egg Net 25R after your first spill · Summit Lift 29R after a fall.
- **Clip moment:** A diving catch of a stranger's Legendary egg.
- **Returns:** D1 daily summit egg · D7 new summit band · D28 limited climber.
- **MVP:** Goat and gecko, marked grip panels, a server-controlled egg bounce, a scripted faller for empty servers.
- **Kill if:** Ride A Pet ships climbing mounts.
- **Risk:** Players may read "For Eggs" as the sixth reskin.

### 3. Ride the Pet Maze
- **First 30 seconds:** On a bunny at a corn-maze gate: jump a wall and grab an egg. A banner reads "Reshuffles in 7:32".
- **Core loop:** One shared maze that swaps layout every 8 minutes. Your pet's move (hop, dig, chew) reaches eggs in dead ends. Carry 3 out and hatch new moves. A farm pays idle coins.
- **Twist:** Every pet's shortcut stays open for the whole server.
- **Painkillers:** Owl Flare 19R at your 3rd dead end · Bell Saver 15R when the bell rings while you carry eggs · Maze Map 35R with 2 minutes left.
- **Clip moment:** A goat chews a hole and the whole server pours through.
- **Returns:** D1 farm coins · D7 new skin and move · D28 season pet.
- **MVP:** 6–8 hand-built layouts, 3 moves.
- **Kill if:** Ride A Pet announces a maze biome.
- **Risk:** The corn skin stops working after Nov 1.

### 4. Steal the Ducklings
- **First 30 seconds:** A nest cracks and three ducklings follow you home through a safe tutorial lane.
- **Core loop:** Nests crack every 60 seconds → ducklings imprint on the nearest player → lead your line home → pond ducks pay coins → bigger nests, rebirth.
- **Twist:** Run through a rival's glowing tether and every duckling behind the cut switches to you.
- **Painkillers:** Loud Quack 19R right after a cut · Nest Alarm 25R when a rare nest is announced · Duck Whistle 99R pass after your first loss.
- **Clip moment:** Snipping a 20-duck line in half.
- **Returns:** D1 pond income · D7 new marsh · D28 rideable Mama Duck.
- **MVP:** One marsh, one mud mutation, followers drawn on each player's device.
- **Kill if:** Shepherd Simulator already has you lead a flock home.
- **Risk:** Hundreds of followers lagging the server.

### 5. Cheat the Dog Show
- **First 30 seconds:** A STAY! prompt at second 5. You lob a squeaky toy behind the judge, the rival beagle bolts after it, and your poodle wins a rosette.
- **Core loop:** An 8-dog show with bots filling empty slots: 5-second groom pick, STAY phase, then throw toys outside the judge's sight cone. Rosettes buy pup crates.
- **Twist:** Cheating is legal until the judge's visible sight cone catches you.
- **Painkillers:** Blinkers 19R right after your dog breaks its stay · Judge's Pardon 25R at your first warning (1 per round) · Pup Crate 49R after 3rd place.
- **Clip moment:** A beagle bolting behind the judge's back.
- **Returns:** D1 daily ribbon · D7 themed class (a costume class in late Oct) · D28 championship.
- **MVP:** 3 breeds sharing one rig, no gait lap, bot fill.
- **Kill if:** an "Illegal ___" copy or a sabotage show has 5K+ players.
- **Risk:** Nobody plays pet shows, so all demand comes from the sabotage hook.

### 6. Ride the Stampede
- **First 30 seconds:** A dust wall of zebras and bison with one glowing elk. You hop back to back and hold on through three bucks. "YOURS!"
- **Core loop:** A shared herd runs a loop around the map. Hop across backs, hold one animal through its buck meter to claim it plus a Herd Egg. Your ranch pays coins.
- **Twist:** The rarest animals run in the dangerous middle of the herd.
- **Painkillers:** Grip Glove 19R when you're bucked off near a rare · Saddle Pack 49R when your ranch is full · Herd Whistle 35R when the biome changes.
- **Clip moment:** Chain-hopping five backs to reach the golden elk.
- **Returns:** D1 ranch coins · D7 new biome route · D28 limited golden species.
- **MVP:** 50 animals on a fixed path timed by a server clock, recolors of one rig.
- **Kill if:** Ride A Pet adds a herd event.
- **Risk:** Jumping onto moving animals feels bad with lag.

### 7. Kick the Egg
- **First 30 seconds:** Kickoff on a mole. You burrow under a defender, pop up and score. The egg-ball wobbles under "HATCHES FOR THE WINNERS".
- **Core loop:** 3v3 with bots filling empty slots. Each mount breaks one rule (burrow, glide, bounce). The egg-ball hatches for the whole winning team.
- **Twist:** The ball is the gacha and your pet is the illegal move.
- **Painkillers:** Hatch It Anyway 25R on the loss screen · Mount Rental 19R at mount select (not in ranked) · 2x Hatch Luck 15R for your next match.
- **Clip moment:** A mole erupting beside the keeper.
- **Returns:** D1 first-win egg · D7 new mount · D28 ranked season.
- **MVP:** 3 abilities on cooldowns, a server-controlled ball, 3v3.
- **Kill if:** a pet or mounted soccer game has 5K+ players.
- **Risk:** Ball feel. Kick For Eggs was killed over exactly this.

### 8. Hunt the Legend Egg
- **First 30 seconds:** You're on a bat when "LEGEND EGG!" sounds. Ping and a ring glows on the ground. A bot's owl beam crosses it, and you ride to the glow.
- **Core loop:** Ride and collect normal eggs. Every 10 minutes a hidden Legend Egg spawns. Each species gives one clue, and nearby riders share a glowing search zone that shrinks with each clue.
- **Twist:** Your pet's species is the information you bring.
- **Painkillers:** Echo Boost 15R when the alarm sounds · Clue Flare 10R when you're alone · Second Sense 19R for an extra clue type.
- **Clip moment:** Three clues snapping into one X.
- **Returns:** D1 Legend streak · D7 new clue species · D28 Mythic egg.
- **MVP:** 2 clue types, bot riders when fewer than 4 players.
- **Kill if:** Ride A Pet's radar already gives distance or direction.
- **Risk:** Too close to Ride A Pet's own loop.

### 9. Ride An Egg
- **First 30 seconds:** Stand on a giant egg and roll it down a mud ramp. The shell stripes brown and cracks into a Mud Pup.
- **Core loop:** Find eggs by radar and roll them home. Distance hatches them, the biomes you crossed set the species, and cracks drop the rarity one tier. Pets pay nest income.
- **Twist:** Hatching comes from skill and route choice, not a timer.
- **Painkillers:** Shell Patch 19R when the egg cracks · Golden Shell 49R before rolling a rare egg · Odometer x2 29R at the start.
- **Clip moment:** Wobbling a Legendary egg across a lava bridge.
- **Returns:** D1 nest income · D7 new biome color · D28 limited route egg.
- **MVP:** A scripted sphere with the rider attached, 3 routes, first crack free.
- **Kill if:** a hamster-ball hatch game has 5K+ players.
- **Risk:** Rolling feels janky on phones.

### 10. Swap Pets Midair
- **First 30 seconds:** A frog launches you to island 1, and its egg hatches a glider. Leap the next gap and swap to the glider in midair.
- **Core loop:** Chain pet moves (jump, glide, grapple) to reach floating egg tiers. Each hatch is a new move that opens a higher tier. Pets at home pay coins.
- **Twist:** Pets are moves, not stat multipliers.
- **Painkillers:** Checkpoint Feather 15R right after a fall · Egg Shield 25R after a bump costs you an egg · Shiny Luck 19R at the hatch.
- **Clip moment:** A jump-glide-grapple chain onto a sky island.
- **Returns:** D1 idle coins · D7 new tier and move · D28 seasonal tier.
- **MVP:** 4 moves, grapple only to glowing points, one-button swap on mobile.
- **Kill if:** any game with 5K+ players has switchable movement pets.
- **Risk:** Tuning chained movement in one month.

### 11. Ride Pets Together
- **First 30 seconds:** An NPC buddy hops onto your mount's back seat and breathes fire on an ice wall. A Twin Nest cracks into two matching dragons.
- **Core loop:** The front rider steers and the back rider fires the mount's ability. Twin Nests need both. Twins level up faster when ridden together.
- **Twist:** Every mount is built for two players.
- **Painkillers:** Hired Rider 19R after 20 seconds with no match · Re-Bond 25R to re-pair a twin you hatched with a stranger · Twin-Egg Luck 25R at a Twin Nest.
- **Clip moment:** Two identical dragons popping out, one for each rider.
- **Returns:** D1 daily Twin Nest · D7 new biome · D28 limited twin pair.
- **MVP:** One two-seat rig, 2 abilities, 1 biome.
- **Kill if:** Ride A Pet already has passenger seats.
- **Risk:** Empty back seats at launch.

## 5. Killed in verification

- **Freeze the Sea!:** TAKEN. Ride A Pet (210.1M visits, 152.9K–195K players) runs the same find eggs → carry home → hatch → weekly biome loop. A pet that freezes water is one weekly update for them.
- **Crew a Giant Pet:** TAKEN. It uses the same Ride A Pet loop (radar eggs, ride, hatch, even a volcano biome). Crew seats are a feature Ride A Pet could patch in.
- **Hush Your Pet:** TAKEN. Rounds where one hunter chases many hiders belong to Forsaken (82–88K). Hide-and-seek games (Hide or OOF! has 803M visits) and sound-hunting horror (A Quiet Place: Deadzone, It Hears You) crowd the rest. October horror is a guess from last year, not a live trend.

## 6. Build order

The 2026 Halloween Spotlight dates are not announced. In 2025 it ran Oct 23–Nov 3. This plan assumes one scripter and one builder working on one game at a time.

1. **Drag the Giant Egg: start now and ship Oct 21–28.**
