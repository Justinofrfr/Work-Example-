## Loop
1. Spawn → FREE GIFT board (like + favorite + group) = +1,500 Speed, +1,500 Strength once.
2. Quarry pit under "BLOCKS" sign → hold E on stones; start carrying 1 block.
3. Haul across desert to the PYRAMID; place in a green highlighted zone that climbs as tiers fill.
4. Each block placed = +1 Coin and +1 on the shared server counter.
5. Gym between trips: benches/arm weights → Strength (carry capacity); treadmills → Speed. Walk in to auto-train; trains while AFK.
6. Completion: cutscene, click for +1 Pyramid; base opens 3 chambers (Waters of Nu pool = 2x training, pool party, treasury/throne); desert resets after ~3 min.
7. Size: 171,700 blocks (Sep 18–23); "around 240,000" in a later/undated revision.

## Stats
Only Speed (walk speed) and Strength (carry capacity). Guide capacity path 1 → 4 → 10–11 per trip. Late-game video: speed 123, capacity 7,335. A rep at 50x takes as long as at 1x but gives 50x. Per-rep gains and caps not found.
