# Build the Pyramid! — rewards, live-ops, growth (research 2026-10-01; snippets only)

## Name collision
An older "Build the Pyramid!" by TestGrounds32 (Jun 18 2025, 25-player servers, Pharaoh boss) exists. Boss/badge mentions may come from that game, not Janitors'.

## Completion
- 171,700 blocks shared per server (older figure ~240,000). 1 coin per delivered block.
- Every contributor gets a flat +1 Pyramid win. No source shows contribution scaling, a minimum, or a last-block bonus.
- Pyramid wins → rank titles (Apprentice → Artisan → Chief Mason @8 → Prophet of Ra → High Priest) and gym unlocks:
| Gym | Pyramids |
|---|---|
| 2x | 1 | 5x | 3 | 10x | 8 | 25x | 15 | 50x | 25 | 75x | 50 | 100x | 100 | Admin | pass only |
- Celebration: short scene of the finished monument → contributors go inside for 3 minutes (blue pool "Waters of Nu", ~2x training gym) → desert resets, new pyramid.
- Tiers: Basic / Silver / Gold / Void / Gem Pyramid, each with its own completion % (likely Update 1.5).
