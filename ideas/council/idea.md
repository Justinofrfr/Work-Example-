# Council shortlist — 22 ideas (from 100). Constraint: solo scripter + builder, free-to-play, MVP in ≤1 month, Robux via dev products/passes.
Note: the developer already owns a working peg-board ("Plinko") ball-drop system and a promo-code system in Roblox Studio.

1. CRACK A GEODE — Buy geodes from a shop that restocks every 5 min → tap to crack → crystal reveal with rarity + stacking mutations → sell or place in a museum case other players can visit. Server-wide "Meteor Geode" events. Luck potions, 2x crack speed, auto-crack.
2. GROW A PEARL — Seed oysters in your lagoon plot → they grow offline → pry open → pearl RNG (color/size/luster). Tide/weather events add mutations. String pearls into jewelry for sell multipliers.
3. BREED A SNAKE — Pair snakes → egg incubator timer → hatch morphs using real ball-python genetics (dominant/recessive/co-dom) as a gacha. Rare morphs are trade/flex items.
4. MAKE A SLIME — Mix ingredients → poke/stretch ASMR → texture/color RNG → sell. Slime-making loop (not just slime-themed RNG).
5. BUILD A KEYBOARD — Open switch/keycap packs → assemble keyboards → typing-sound ASMR → typing races to earn. Rides the "+1 Speed Keyboard Escape" meme.
6. CATCH A FALLING STAR — Scheduled server-wide star showers → run/jump with a net → star RNG (rarity + mutations) → star jar collection, passive light income.
7. SMASH A PIÑATA — Whack piñatas → candy/toy RNG shower → upgrade bats → giant co-op server piñata every 10 min.
8. VENDING MACHINE EMPIRE — Place vending machines around a map → restock → earn passively → rare-item "mystery slot". Rides a huge TikTok vending-machine-restock content trend.
9. FISH MARKET AUCTION — Dawn auction: players blind-bid on crates of catch → reveal → sell at their own stall. Storage-Hunters-style bid drama.
10. CLEAN A CRIME SCENE — Scrub blood/evidence (cleaning ASMR) and dispose of evidence before the cops arrive; co-op crew; tension timer. Based on Crime Scene Cleaner (Steam 2024).
11. OPEN A CARD SHOP — Open TCG packs → grade → price → sell to NPCs/players; card-shop-owner tycoon.
12. RUN A CLAW MACHINE ARCADE — Play claw machines (skill + RNG) for plush collection with mutations; own an arcade other players play in.
13. RACE A SNAIL — Breed/train snails with genetics → server-wide race every 5 min → prize pool of in-game currency (no betting). Meme-able.
14. DROP A BALL — Peg-board ball-drop incremental/roguelike (arcade framing, no casino look): drop balls → bounce pegs → multiplier slots → upgrade pegs/balls → rebirth. Ball-type RNG. (Dev already has the peg system.)
15. MERGE BATTLE — Suika-style fruit-drop merge game but 1v1/4-player PvP: merges send junk to opponents' jars.
16. 50 PLAYER MUSICAL CHAIRS — Massive parlor game: music stops → sprint for chairs → eliminated; chair/emote cosmetics; rides the 50 Player BINGO wave.
17. WATER GUN ASSASSIN — Senior-Assassin TikTok trend: every player in a big server gets a secret target; tag them with a water gun, inherit their target; last one standing.
18. TELEPHONE DRAWING — Gartic-Phone chain: write → draw → guess → draw…, hilarious reveal at the end.
19. BLIND AUCTION PARTY — 10-min party rounds: 20 players blind-bid on mystery boxes; items revealed; richest net worth wins; persistent collection of won items.
20. PICO PARTY OBBY — Co-op obby with shared controls/physics tethers and engineered troll moments (PICO PARK style).
21. LIFE SIM (BitLife-style) — Age up year by year, random life events, choices, careers, relationships; share wild life outcomes; UI-heavy, cheap to build.
22. CAMERA ROOM SABOTEUR — Multicam observation-duty anomaly hunt (report anomalies on cameras), but one player is secretly the saboteur causing anomalies; social deduction.
